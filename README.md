## Overview

This mod adds a powerful in-game menu panel that provides instant access to essential cheat options, allowing you to customize your Civilization VI experience with ease.

## Key Features

### Resources & Economy
- **Add +100 Faith** - Boost your religious power
- **Add +100 Gold** - Enhance your treasury
- **Add +5 Envoy** - Strengthen diplomatic relations

### City Management
- **Complete Production** - Instantly finish city projects
- **Add +1 Population** - Grow your cities
- **Add FULL Loyalty** - Secure city allegiance
- **Raze Selected City** - Remove unwanted settlements

### Research & Development
- **Complete Current Tech Research** - Advance technology instantly
- **Complete Current Civic Research** - Progress government policies
- **Complete ALL Research** - Unlock everything at once

### Military & Units
- **Restore Unit Movement & Attack** - Refresh unit actions
- **Restore Unit Health** - Heal damaged units
- **Give +1 Promotion** - Upgrade unit abilities
- **Add +5 Movement** - Increase unit mobility
- **Duplicate Selected Unit** - Clone units (excludes Great Persons & Religious Units)

### Game Progression
- **Add +10 Era Points** - Accelerate era advancement
- **Add +1 Governor Title Point** - Expand governor capabilities
- **Toggle Map Observer** - Enhanced map visibility

## Current Modifications

## Requirements

- Civilization VI (Base Game)
- Compatible with most DLCs and expansions (Optional)

## Installation

1. Download the mod files
2. Place in your Civilization VI Mods folder
3. Enable in the game's Additional Content menu
4. Enjoy enhanced gameplay!
